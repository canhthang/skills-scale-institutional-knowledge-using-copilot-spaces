# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

Security & Compliance Lead
Role Summary
The Security & Compliance Lead ensures that projects meet required security, privacy, and compliance standards while balancing delivery goals and team constraints.

Responsibilities
Review architecture and implementation decisions for security risks
Ensure compliance requirements are captured early in planning
Coordinate security reviews and remediation work
Support risk assessment and mitigation across the project lifecycle
Goals
Reduce security and compliance risk
Embed secure-by-default practices into delivery
Support safe, trustworthy releases
Typical Communication
Design reviews and architecture check-ins
Security risk discussions with PMs and engineering leads
Compliance and remediation updates for stakeholders
Interaction with Existing Roles
Works with Developers to validate implementation patterns
Coordinates with Project Managers on risk tracking and mitigation plans
Partners with Product Managers to ensure customer and regulatory requirements are reflected in scope
Technical Lead / Architect
Role Summary
The Technical Lead / Architect provides technical direction, aligns implementation decisions to system goals, and helps the team make scalable and maintainable choices.

Responsibilities
Define technical standards and architectural direction
Guide design trade-offs and technical constraints
Support prioritization of technical debt and implementation decisions
Mentor developers and help remove technical blockers
Goals
Produce robust, maintainable technical solutions
Align implementation with long-term product goals
Improve delivery confidence and system quality
Typical Communication
Architecture reviews
Technical planning and design discussions
Cross-team dependency and integration alignment
Interaction with Existing Roles
Works with Developers to clarify implementation choices
Supports Project Managers on milestones, risks, and dependencies
Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs
QA Lead / Quality Owner
Role Summary
The QA Lead ensures that quality standards are defined, tested, and sustained throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Responsibilities
Define test strategy and quality gate criteria
Review acceptance criteria and release readiness
Coordinate regression and validation activities
Identify quality risks and mitigation actions
Goals
Improve release confidence
Reduce defects reaching production
Ensure acceptance criteria are measurable and validated
Typical Communication
Test planning and QA check-ins
Release readiness reviews
Defect triage and validation updates
Interaction with Existing Roles
Partners with Developers on test planning and defect resolution
Communicates release quality risk to Project Managers and stakeholders
Aligns quality standards with Product Manager acceptance expectations
Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison
Role Summary
The Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaison ensures delivery remains aligned to customer needs, stakeholder expectations, and communication commitments.

Responsibilities
Capture stakeholder feedback and concerns
Coordinate updates and communication cadence
Support rollout readiness and adoption discussions
Help translate customer feedback into actionable product decisions
Goals
Strengthen stakeholder trust
Maintain customer impact visibility
Improve adoption and satisfaction
Typical Communication
Stakeholder updates
Customer feedback reviews
Milestone and release communications
Interaction with Existing Roles
Works with Product Managers on customer value and feedback loops
Supports Project Managers on communication planning
Helps Developers understand real-world user impact and edge cases
Business Analyst / Operations Partner
Role Summary
The Business Analyst / Operations Partner helps connect business process realities with delivery work, especially around operational readiness and rollout success.

Responsibilities
Clarify requirements and process workflows
Identify operational dependencies for launches
Support documentation and process readiness
Help coordinate business-side rollout planning
Goals
Improve rollout readiness and adoption
Reduce operational surprises after release
Support smooth cross-functional implementation
Typical Communication
Requirements clarifications and process validation
Rollout planning and readiness reviews
Operational dependency tracking
Interaction with Existing Roles
Works closely with Project Managers to align scope and sequencing
Supports Product Managers on market and business requirements
Coordinates with Developers and QA on acceptance and readiness criteria
